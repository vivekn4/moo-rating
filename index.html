🐄 Moo Rating

Milk the herd. Mind the mood. Gwendolyn is watching.

  • Click a cow (or press Space) to milk it.
  • Slower is better: more than 4 clicks a second stresses the herd, drops the Moo Rating, and the cows remember.
  • Spend Milk on a better milker, more cows, and feeding the herd.
  • Every 45 seconds the herd forces a choice: appease, ignore, or exploit.
  • Reach 5,000 total Milk to see your ending — Pastoral, Gigafarm, or somewhere in between.

Tip: feed the herd when the Moo Rating falls — a calm herd milks better.
